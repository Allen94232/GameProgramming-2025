# Campus Dash 🚴‍♂️

[![Play on itch.io](https://img.shields.io/badge/Play%20on-itch.io-FA5C5C?style=for-the-badge&logo=itch.io&logoColor=white)](https://allen94232.itch.io/campusdash)

A fast-paced campus racing game where every second counts! Race through campus on your bicycle, dodging obstacles and managing your mood to reach class before time runs out.

## 🎮 Game Description

In a campus with strict attendance rules, being late means failing the course. You glance at the time and realize you only have a few minutes until your next class starts!

The campus instantly transforms into a racetrack. You must jump on your bike and race at full speed through campus, avoiding various obstacles: those perpetually clueless pigeons, groups of students chatting in the middle of the path, and more. Master your timing and skills to not just reach the destination before the countdown ends, but to achieve the highest score.

## 🎯 Game Features

- **Time-Based Challenge**: Race against the clock to reach your class on time
- **Quick Decision Making**: Dodge obstacles and NPCs in split seconds
- **Interactive Environment**: Use your mouse to interact with objects in the world
- **Mood System**: Manage your mood to maintain optimal speed
- **Multiple Levels**: Tutorial and two main levels with leaderboards
- **Competitive Gameplay**: Global leaderboards to compete with other players

## 🕹️ Controls

- **W / S / Left Shift**: Accelerate / Brake
- **A / D**: Turn left / Right
- **Spacebar**: Ring bicycle bell (scares away small animals)
- **Mouse**: Click and drag to interact with environmental objects

## 📋 Gameplay Elements

### HUD Information
- **Timer**: Top-left corner - reach the finish line before time runs out
- **Mood Bar**: Affects your movement speed - keep it high to move faster
- **Minimap**: Shows the general direction to your destination

### Objects & Obstacles

| Object | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| 💰 **Coins** | Collecting money makes you happy, increases mood |
| 🚧 **Traffic Cones & Trash Cans** | Obstacles - avoid collisions |
| 🚶 **Pedestrians** | Wandering NPCs - hitting them makes both of you upset |
| 🐦 **Birds** | Ring your bell to scare them away before colliding |
| 💩 **Bird Droppings** | Watch for shadows! Slows you down and decreases mood |
| 📷 **Speed Cameras** | Get fined for speeding over 20 - use spray paint to block them |
| 💦 **Fire Hydrants** | Click to turn them off - getting wet is no fun |
| 🚗 **Cars** | Safety first - avoid vehicles on the road |
| 🏁 **Flag** | The finish line - reach it to complete the level |

## 🏆 Scoring System

Your final score is based on:
- Time remaining when you reach the goal
- Mood level maintained throughout the race
- Coins collected during the run

Compete for the top spots on the global leaderboards for each level!
